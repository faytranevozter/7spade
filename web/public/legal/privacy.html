This policy describes how Seven Spade (“the Service”), operated by Fahrur Rifai, handles information when you use our multiplayer card game in a browser at https://spade.my.id. The same backend practices apply to our mobile app; the canonical public Privacy Policy URL is https://spade.my.id/privacy.

1. Who we are

Seven Spade is a real-time multiplayer card game. The website and mobile clients talk to our game API and WebSocket services. The data controller for the production Service at https://spade.my.id is Fahrur Rifai, contactable at support@spade.my.id. If you operate your own self-hosted backend deployment, you are the data controller for that deployment.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account & profile (registered users)

When you create or link a registered account, we store in our database:

  • Email address (optional for some OAuth paths such as Telegram)
  • Password hash if you register with email (we do not store your password in plain text)
  • Display name and unique username
  • Linked OAuth provider records (provider name, provider user id, provider email if supplied, avatar URL) for providers you choose (e.g. GitHub, Google, Telegram)
  • Email verification timestamp when you complete verification
  • Account creation time

Guests do not get a durable account row. Playing as guest issues a short-lived access token that carries a temporary id and display name only (no email, username, password, friends, stats, or refresh cookie).

2.2 Gameplay & social data

  • Finished games and per-player results (display name, penalties, rank, win flag, optional user id, bot flag), including room name metadata where recorded
  • Lifetime and seasonal stats for registered players (game counts, ratings, streaks, XP, levels, achievements, daily-login progress, event participation, and related aggregates)
  • Per-game rating and XP event history for registered players
  • Detailed result cards and full move/hand replay data for a limited number of recent finished games (older detail/replay rows are pruned on save)
  • Room membership while a room exists (user id, display name, invite code, visibility, room settings, host kicks so a kicked player cannot rejoin that room)
  • Friendships for registered users (pending, accepted, or blocked relationships between user ids)
  • Owned and equipped cosmetic skins, how a skin was earned, and the applicable event or unlock rule
  • Moderation information when action is taken on an account, including suspension status, reason, expiry, and an administrative audit record of the action
  • Live presence for registered users while connected (online and optional current room id), stored in Redis for up to 60 seconds without a heartbeat and removed on a normal disconnect - not written for guests
  • Live room snapshots in Redis while a room is active (roster, game board/hands, moves, timers, config), with a one-hour expiry refreshed on each save so inactive rooms expire

Public surfaces such as leaderboards and player profile pages may show display name, username, avatar (from a linked OAuth provider when present), and gameplay stats for registered players who appear there. Registered users may also be findable by username search for the friends feature.

2.3 Browser & session data

  • Access JWT in browser sessionStorage for the current tab (includes subject id, display name, guest flag, and optional avatar URL claim)
  • HttpOnly refresh_token cookie for registered sessions (about 30 days); the server stores only a hash of the refresh token in the database. This cookie is used solely to keep you signed in.
  • Temporary invite code in sessionStorage when you open an invite link before joining
  • Local preferences in browser localStorage (sound mute, card-animation speed, tutorial seen/skipped status)

We do not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or location for core gameplay. We do not currently ship a third-party crash/analytics SDK in the web client.

2.4 Technical and operational data

When you contact or use the Service, our servers and infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, user-agent strings, request times, requested URLs, request identifiers, error details, and security events in access or application logs. We use this information to deliver requests, diagnose failures, secure the Service, enforce rate limits, and investigate abuse. Administrative access and moderation actions are additionally recorded in restricted audit logs, which may contain the acting administrator, reason, affected resource, before/after state, IP address, user agent, outcome, and time.

2.5 Short-lived server cache (Redis)

In addition to presence and live rooms, the API may temporarily store:

  • OAuth login state / PKCE verifier for up to 10 minutes
  • Hashed password-reset tokens for up to 15 minutes
  • Hashed email-verification tokens for up to 24 hours
  • Per-email or per-user rate-limit counters for recovery and similar actions

3. How we use information

  • Provide multiplayer gameplay, matchmaking, rooms, spectating, and replays
  • Authenticate you and keep sessions secure (JWT access tokens, refresh rotation, logout)
  • Maintain leaderboards, seasons, achievements, rating/XP, login streaks, events, skins, and game history
  • Enable friends, username search, invites, and account recovery (password reset / email verification)
  • Improve reliability and prevent abuse (logs, rate limits, room kicks, friendship blocks, and moderation)
  • Respond to your privacy and account requests (see Your rights)

4. Legal bases (where applicable)

If GDPR or similar laws apply: performance of a contract (providing the game), legitimate interests (security, anti-abuse, product improvement, public leaderboards as part of the game), and consent where required (e.g. optional processing that requires consent). We do not currently use personal data for advertising or marketing.

5. Sharing

We share data with:

  • Infrastructure and object-storage providers that host our web application, API, PostgreSQL database, Redis, real-time WebSocket services, logs, backups where configured, and public cosmetic assets
  • Google, GitHub, or Telegram when you choose that provider to sign in or link an account. Their own privacy terms govern the information they process, and we receive the profile identifiers they return.
  • Email delivery providers when email delivery is configured for password reset or verification messages
  • Other players in the same room or on public leaderboards/profiles (display name, play state, and stats as shown in the product UI)
  • Authorized administrators and service operators who need access for support, security, moderation, maintenance, or legal compliance
  • Authorities or other parties when required by law or necessary to protect rights and safety

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Retention

  • Registered account data, friendships, stats, login progress, achievements, skins, event progress, and game history summaries: while the account exists, unless a longer period is needed for security or legal obligations
  • Guest identity: only inside the guest JWT until it expires after 7 days or you discard the tab/token; guests are not given a permanent user row
  • Move-by-move replay and detailed face-down result data: limited to the latest 20 finished games under the current production configuration. Older details are pruned when new games are saved. Basic game history rows may remain longer.
  • Live presence: removed on disconnect or within 60 seconds without a heartbeat
  • Live room snapshots: removed when the room is deleted or after one hour without a refreshed save
  • OAuth state: 10 minutes; password-reset token hashes: 15 minutes; email-verification token hashes: 24 hours
  • Access JWT: until its 7-day expiry or you clear sessionStorage; refresh cookie and database hash: until logout, rotation, password reset, revocation, or its 30-day expiry
  • Security, infrastructure, and administrative audit logs: for the period reasonably necessary to secure, operate, and investigate the Service, or longer where law requires. The exact period can vary by log source.
  • Browser localStorage preferences: until you clear site data

When account deletion is finalized after the grace period, the active database deletes account-linked records through its relational deletion rules. Historical game seats are anonymized as described below. Where routine infrastructure backups exist, deleted data may remain in a protected backup until that backup expires and is not restored except for disaster recovery.

7. Security

We use industry-standard transport security (HTTPS / WSS). Passwords are stored hashed. Refresh tokens are stored hashed server-side and sent to the browser only as an HttpOnly cookie (SameSite=Strict) on the web client. Access tokens live in sessionStorage for the tab. One-time email tokens are stored as hashes in Redis. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Access to administrative tools is permission-controlled and sensitive administrative actions are audited.

8. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region). Do not use the Service if you are under the applicable age without parental consent where required. If you believe we have collected data from a child in violation of applicable law, contact support@spade.my.id and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

9. Your choices

  • Play as guest (no durable account; limited features - no friends, stats, events, or skins account surface)
  • Edit display name (registered users)
  • Log out (clears the in-tab access token and instructs the server to clear the refresh cookie / revoke that refresh token)
  • Mute sound, change motion speed, and dismiss or complete the tutorial (stored only in your browser)
  • Exercise the rights described below, including self-serve account deletion on your profile

10. Your rights and account deletion

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, to object to certain processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

How to make a request: for most rights, email support@spade.my.id from the address on your account (or include your username / user id and enough detail to verify ownership). State what you want (e.g. access copy, correction). For account deletion you can use self-serve delete on My profile, or email us as a fallback.

Account deletion (self-serve): registered users can schedule deletion from My profile > Danger zone. Email/password accounts must confirm with the current password; OAuth-only accounts may confirm without a password step-up in this version. After you schedule:

  • A 7-day grace period begins. You may cancel deletion from My profile while the schedule is pending (you can stay signed in for this purpose).
  • Refresh sessions are revoked when deletion is scheduled so other devices must sign in again.
  • After the grace period ends, a periodic cleanup job hard-deletes your active user account and linked OAuth provider rows, friendships, stats, achievements, login progress, skin entitlements, rating/XP events, and session tokens.
  • Historical game seats remain for other players' history but are labeled Deleted User and detached from your user id (anonymized gameplay records are retained).

Guests have no durable server account: discard your local token (log out) to stop using the Service as that guest identity. We may refuse or limit a request only where allowed by law (e.g. we cannot verify you control the account, or retention is required for security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations).
