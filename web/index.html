Seven Spade

Seven Spade is a free, real-time multiplayer card game based on the classic game of 7s. Players build suit sequences from the sevens outward and try to finish with the lowest penalty score.

Play the classic card game online

Play in your browser with friends or bots, create or join multiplayer rooms, practice against bots, watch live games, or compete in ranked play. Custom rooms support 2-8 players, teams, multiple decks, and alternate scoring. Earn XP, achievements, daily rewards, event progress, and cosmetic skins; add friends, view profiles, and revisit game history and move-by-move replays. You can play as a guest or create an account using email, Google, GitHub, or Telegram sign-in.
